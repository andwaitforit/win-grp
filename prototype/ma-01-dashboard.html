    Estimate assumptions

    GRPs aren't in the filings, so they're estimated from spend. Change a cost per point and every number on the page updates.

    GRPs ≈ gross × (1 − commission) ÷ CPP. Outside the lowest-unit-rate window, CPP × 1.2. Range = estimate ± band.

    Questions for reviewers

    1. Is weekly GRPs by market the number you'd check first? If not, what is?
    2. Would you act on an estimate shown as a ±20% range? What would make you trust it more?
    3. For a new-filing alert, what do you need to see before you call the media buyer?
    4. Albany GRPs cover the whole market, and only about 1 in 10 of its TV homes are in Berkshire County. Should we show in-district GRPs instead?
    5. What's missing? Cable, digital, outside groups, creative?
